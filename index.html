Engineering Excellence

The Rice Electric Vehicle Team (REV) is a student-run nonprofit organization dedicated to the design, construction, and racing of sustainable vehicles. Building on a legacy of efficiency, we are now undertaking our most ambitious challenge yet: designing a road-legal, multi-passenger solar car to compete in the American Solar Challenge.

Meet The Team

The American Solar Challenge

The ASC is the ultimate test of grit and engineering. It is a multi-day, 1,500+ mile road rally across the United States driven entirely by solar energy.

Before hitting the open road, our vehicle must survive the Formula Sun Grand Prix, a grueling track race that tests handling and braking. Our goal is to demonstrate practical, solar-powered mobility for the real world.

Our Teams

Electrical Team

The nerve center of the car. We design custom PCBs, manage the battery management system (BMS), and ensure efficient power distribution from solar arrays to the motor.

Mechanical Team

The muscle and bones. We focus on aerodynamics, chassis design, suspension geometry, and composite manufacturing to build a vehicle that is lightweight yet structural.

Business & Operations

The fuel that keeps us running. We manage team logistics, corporate sponsorships, public relations, and finance to ensure we have the resources to compete.
