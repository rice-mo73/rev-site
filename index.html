Engineering Excellence

The Rice Electric Vehicle Team (REV) is an entirely student-run organization dedicated to the design, construction, and racing of sustainable vehicles. Building on a legacy of efficiency, we are now undertaking our most ambitious challenge yet: designing a road-legal, multi-passenger solar car to compete in the American Solar Challenge.

Meet The Team

The American Solar Challenge

The ASC is the ultimate test of grit and engineering. It is a multi-day, 1,500+ mile road rally across the United States driven entirely by solar energy.

Before hitting the open road, our vehicle must survive the Formula Sun Grand Prix, a grueling track race that tests handling and braking. Our goal is to demonstrate practical, solar-powered mobility for the real world.
